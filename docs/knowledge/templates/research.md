# Research record template

Copy this template to a descriptive Markdown filename; fill every field before acceptance.

- Question and workstream:
- Hypothesis and alternative explanation:
- Recorded date, author/reviewer and repository revision:
- Source IDs (`source:GW-...`), original URLs, publication/access dates and revisions:
- Evidence status (`candidate`/`design`/`implemented`/`reported`/`observed`):
- Source rights/license; quote/link/redistribution limits:
- Curriculum notice, stage/grade applicability and effective dates (or unknown):
- Prior experiments and decisions; contradictions and missing evidence:
- Synthetic dataset/fixture and exclusions:
- Reproduction command, environment and model/provider revision:
- Observations separated from interpretation:
- Outcome, failure analysis, uncertainty and next falsification step:
- Privacy review: no child data, private history, secrets or device identifiers:
- Child-safety review: non-diagnostic, scaffold, age fit, uncertainty and parent choice:
- Proposed decision and review status (proposed/accepted/rejected/superseded):
- Linked ADR and explicit acceptance/revisit criteria:

Append source/experiment IDs rather than rewriting old ledger entries. Optional LLM synthesis is
an attributed draft requiring human source and safety review; it cannot accept its own proposal.
